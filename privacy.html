Shaker MSI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Shaker MSI respects your privacy. This policy describes the limited data handling associated with our official social-media publishing integrations.

Information we process

Where a user authorizes a platform integration, we may process the account identifiers, permissions, and publishing data that the platform provides to deliver the authorized function. We do not request or store account passwords.

How information is used

Information is used only to publish, schedule, manage, or analyze Shaker MSI content on the platform for which it was authorized. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are not shared in public content or chat messages. We retain information only as long as necessary for the authorized integration or legal obligations.

Third-party platforms

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and other platforms operate under their own privacy policies. Their independent handling of data is governed by those policies.

Your choices

You may revoke an integration through the applicable platform settings. For privacy questions, contact us through @shaker.msi on Instagram.
